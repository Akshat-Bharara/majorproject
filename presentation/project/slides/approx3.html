[Name 3] · Part 3

The third result of the paper is an O(log n)-time approximation for k = 3 using the four extreme points. We implemented it and compared its answer with the exact optimum. The proven ratio is about 0.354. On 20,000 random inputs the worst ratio was 0.73, and even an adversarial search designed to make it fail only got down to 0.61. So the guarantee is safe, and it looks like a better ratio could be proved. We leave that as an open question.
