Brief tour of the literature. The original paper (orange) gave the first FPT algorithm and an O(n^4 k^2) dynamic program; its conference version appeared at CALDAM 2023. Tkachenko and Wang later gave faster algorithms using different techniques. Our main exact algorithm is O(n^3 log k log n), which beats O(n^4 k^2) by a factor of about n·k² and the SWAT 2026 bound by a factor log n / log k. For unimodal inputs we get near-linear time.
