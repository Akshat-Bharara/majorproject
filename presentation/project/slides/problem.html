THE PROBLEM

Pick k points that are as far apart as possible

K-DISPERSION

Given n points in convex position and an integer k, choose k of them so that the smallest distance between two chosen points, δ(S), is as large as possible.

Example from the paper: place k oil-storage plants on the coast of a convex island so that an accident at one plant cannot reach another.

Why convex position? For general point sets the problem is NP-hard. On the boundary of a convex region it becomes polynomial.

Definition: we have n points on the boundary of a convex polygon. We choose k of them. The quality of a choice is the smallest distance between two chosen points; we want to maximise it. The optimal value is called r-star. Application from the original paper: oil plants on a convex island’s coast. In general position the problem is NP-hard; in convex position it can be solved in polynomial time, and the question is how fast.
