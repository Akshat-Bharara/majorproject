Exact k-Dispersion for Points in Convex Position

Revisiting Singireddy, Basappa and Mitchell (Discrete Applied Mathematics, 2026): a faster exact algorithm, a careful check of the original algorithms, and a near-linear algorithm for a special case.

Presenter 1 opens. One sentence on the problem: from n points on the boundary of a convex region, pick k that are as far apart as possible. We build on the 2026 Discrete Applied Mathematics paper by Singireddy, Basappa and Mitchell, which we call “the original paper”. Our project does three things, one per presenter: a faster exact algorithm, a verification of the original algorithms, and a much faster algorithm for an important special case.
