% Abstract
\newpage
\begin{titlepage}
\pagestyle{empty}
\begin{center}
\large \textbf{ABSTRACT}
\end{center}

\begin{singlespacing}
Given $n$ points in convex position in the plane and an integer $k$, the
\emph{$k$-dispersion} problem asks for $k$ of the points whose minimum pairwise
distance is as large as possible. It models the placement of facilities that
must be kept apart, such as transmitters or hazardous plants along a coastline.
Singireddy, Basappa and Mitchell (Discrete Applied Mathematics, 2026) gave a
fixed-parameter algorithm that grows a solution from one point in both
directions along the hull; its correctness rests on a claim whose published
proof is informal. Tkachenko and Wang (SWAT 2026) gave an $O(n^3\log^2n)$
algorithm.

This project makes three contributions. First, we prove the claim of
Singireddy et al.\ for $k\le4$, which makes their algorithm correct for these
values of $k$; for every $k$ we show that the first step of the search can be
made safely, and we give point sets showing that natural rules for choosing the
starting point fail for $k=5,6,7$. Along the way we show that the structural
properties that make $k=3$ easy all fail for $k\ge4$. Second, we identify the
\emph{arc property}---for every point, the points far from it are contiguous
along the hull---under which a single greedy chain is exact; for unimodal point
sets this gives an $O(n\log^2n)$ expected-time algorithm that solves instances
with a million points in seconds. Third, a Delaunay certificate for
well-separated sets yields a simple exact algorithm that decides the problem in
$O(n^3\log k)$ time and solves it in $O(n^3\log k\log n)$ time.

All algorithms are implemented in C++ with exact integer arithmetic and were
validated against exhaustive search on thousands of instances.

\vspace*{12pt}
\noindent \textit{Keywords}: $k$-dispersion, obnoxious facility location,
convex position, Delaunay triangulation, dynamic programming, greedy
algorithms, fixed-parameter algorithms, computational geometry.
\end{singlespacing}
\end{titlepage}
