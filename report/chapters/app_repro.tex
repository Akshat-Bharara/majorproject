\section{Reproducing the results}\label{app:repro}

All code, instances, counterexamples and scripts are in the project
repository. The C++ code needs a C++17 compiler; the scripts need
Python~3 (the verification scripts use only the standard library).

\paragraph{General algorithms.} In \code{convex/}, \code{make} builds the
library tests and the benchmark drivers; the self-test compares the capped
program, the baselines and exhaustive search on random instances, and
\code{make\_tables.py} turns the raw results in \code{convex/results/} into
the tables and plots of \cref{ch:results}. The counterexamples of
\cref{thm:tightpair} are verified by
\code{convex/experiments/check\_counterexamples.py}.

\paragraph{Arc property.} In \code{arc/}, \code{arc\_tests} checks the
greedy chain and the decision procedure against brute force, \code{pybrute.py}
is an independent Python check, \code{check\_hypotheses.py} verifies the
recorded counterexamples to weaker hypotheses, and \code{arc\_bench} produces
the scaling experiments.

\paragraph{Search tree.} \code{sbm/} re-implements the algorithms
of~\citet{sbm}; \code{fpt/k4check.cpp} and \code{fpt/firststep.cpp} test the
statements of \cref{sec:sbm}, and \code{fpt/check\_roots.py} verifies the
root-rule counterexamples of \cref{prop:rootrules} in exact arithmetic.
