\section{Point sets without tight pairs}\label{app:instances}

\Cref{tab:instances} lists the instances behind \cref{thm:tightpair} for
$k=5,6,7$, together with two small instances that separate weaker properties
from $(\mathrm{TP}_k)$. All of them are contained in
\code{convex/data/counterexamples.json} and are verified by
\code{convex/experiments/check_counterexamples.py} by enumerating all
$k$-subsets in exact integer arithmetic.
